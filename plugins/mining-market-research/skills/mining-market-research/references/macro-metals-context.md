# Macro and metals context for fusion

Perform targeted real-time host web research during fusion unless the user
excludes web/macro or capability/permission is unavailable. Do not assume the
stock MCP provides metal series, macro feeds or market daily reports.
No new MCP tool or separate metals Skill is required.

1. Identify actual commodity, geography, operating-cost, currency and financing
   exposures from verified company evidence. Select only relevant channels.
2. Search relevant metal moves and supply/demand, energy, FX, financing or
   geopolitical developments for the analysis window. An explorer's funding
   environment differs from a producer's operating margins.
3. Open reliable sources for material claims; prefer original exchange,
   official statistics, issuer and benchmark publications for numbers.
   Search snippets are discovery aids, not final proof. Date and cite claims.
4. Connect external change -> commodity/cost/funding channel -> actual company
   exposure -> support or alternative explanation for the event/price behavior.
   No generic geopolitical paragraph or mechanical list of every commodity.

For historical events, search the historical period. Label later evidence as
hindsight. Record data cutoff independently for news, stock and macro sources.
Different markets close at different times: avoid future benchmark observations
when explaining a stock close.
In particular, a same-calendar-date London/LME fixing can occur after the ASX
close. Do not use that later fixing to argue that copper did/did not drive the
earlier Australian session. Verify the benchmark timestamp and use an available
prior observation, or limit the comparison to explicitly non-synchronous context.
Date-matched endpoints alone do not establish information available to traders.

Distinguish spot/futures/benchmark, contract maturity/roll, currency, unit and
timestamp. Without comparable sourced series use qualitative context, not an
invented relative return, beta or causal share of the stock move.
Existing accessible market summaries may guide discovery; retain original
sources and dates and distinguish their opinions from facts. Do not call
get_company_report as a market-report lookup or assume a daily-report tool exists.

If network or a needed source is unavailable, explicitly narrow attribution
and deliver available evidence. Never bypass NEWS_ACCESS restrictions with
web research or silently drop requested news facets. The macro search component
does not reset the shared news-body budget or authorize unrelated issuer crawling.
