# Mining analysis priorities

Maintainer-editable defaults for fusion analysis, not a separate Skill.
Select relevant company AND event rows; do not turn every row into a mandatory
section or fetch unrelated evidence. Explicit user scope takes precedence.
These are attention priorities, not empirical claims about every miner.

## Universal defaults
- Establish actual scale, liquidity, project concentration and development stage.
  Never presume small capitalization merely from a mining label.
- Evaluate volume relative to its own baseline AND absolute activity; inspect
  lead-up, event window and persistence. Volume-leading-price is a hypothesis
  to examine, not a stock-exchange rule or predictive guarantee.
- Ask what is new relative to prior public information and what economic
  variable changes. Seek disconfirming evidence as well as support.
- Missing news means no matches in the searched scope. Missing bars do not
  establish a halt. Publication time does not prove first disclosure.
- Disclosed director transactions need actual transaction/disclosure dates.
  Unusual volume alone never proves insider trading, institutional buying,
  accumulation, net inflows or manipulation.
- No matching same-day announcement is not positive evidence of profit-taking,
  portfolio rebalancing or informed trading. These remain possible explanations
  unless independently supported; do not call one the most likely cause merely
  because the news search was empty or the date was month-end.

## Company type
For each type: applicability | priority evidence | judgment channel | prohibited inference.

| Type | Priority evidence | Judgment channel | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Drill location, widths/true-width status, continuity, ownership, cash and next work program | Whether geological understanding and ability to test it change | A best intercept proves an economic mine; metal upside is current profit |
| Developer/construction | Study assumptions, resource categories, recoveries, permits, remaining capex, funding and schedule | Which conditions to construction/production changed | A permit means fully funded or ready to produce |
| Producer | Production, grades, recovery, unit costs, guidance, outages, hedging | Cash generation and operational risk, considering commodity/FX/cost exposure | Higher output always improves margins |
| Royalty/streaming | Contract terms, operators, asset concentration and deliveries | Economic entitlement and counterparty/project risk | Apply a mine operator's direct cost structure unchanged |
| Diversified/multi-asset | Relevant segment contribution, portfolio exposures, capital allocation | Materiality to the whole company | One project's event determines group value |
| Mixed/unknown | Verified asset-level stages and available facts | Combine applicable rows and disclose uncertainty | Force one category or invent missing financials |

Classification evidence may come from existing verified context, current
announcements and official company material. Do not repeatedly reclassify an
unchanged issuer; revisit when new facts show a material stage change.

## Event overlay
| Event | Verify | Interpret jointly with market evidence | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financing | Proposed vs closed, discount reference date, warrants, use of proceeds, funding need | Liquidity improvement versus dilution and remaining milestones | Financing is automatically bullish/bearish; trading above the placement price proves investor acceptance |
| Drilling/resource | New versus repeated results, geometry, continuity, category and study context | Changed project understanding versus market attention | Grade alone determines value |
| Study/permit | Assumptions, conditions, remaining approvals, capex and timing | Which uncertainty is reduced and which remains | Headline approval eliminates execution risk |
| Production/guidance | Prior guidance, actuals, costs, recoveries and one-offs | Operational delta versus metal and input-cost changes | A beat without a documented comparison |
| Acquisition/royalty/offtake | Consideration, ownership, retained obligations and economic rights | What is gained versus surrendered or financed | Deal size alone indicates value creation |

## Special situations
Low baseline liquidity, placements/warrants, corporate actions, index changes,
verified halts/reopenings and overlapping announcements may change the volume
interpretation. Check only relevant, evidenced explanations. Do not substitute
rumors for findings or assume that a price query proves wrongdoing.
Higher volume establishes greater observed activity, not that the market has
fully priced, endorsed or rejected a transaction. Express such interpretations
as hypotheses with competing explanations and an explicit observation window.

## Maintenance
For additions retain four elements: applicability, evidence, interpretation,
and prohibited inference. Prefer narrow corrections from real tests; avoid
unverified exchange stereotypes, fixed trading signals and universal scores.
